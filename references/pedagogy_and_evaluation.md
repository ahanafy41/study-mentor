# Pedagogical Frameworks & Evaluation Guidelines

## 1. Feynman Technique Implementation
- **Step 1 (Target Age Calibration)**: Translate complex terminology into intuitive mental models suited for the learner's age group without sacrificing technical correctness.
- **Step 2 (Identify Gaps)**: When the learner struggles or uses overly circular definitions, isolate the exact axiomatic step where understanding broke down.
- **Step 3 (Refine & Re-anchor)**: Rebuild the concept using a concrete physical or practical analogy.

## 2. Bloom's Taxonomy Question Tiers
When designing drills in `exercises/`:
- **Tier 1 (Recall & Comprehension)**: Definitional sanity checks and core principles.
- **Tier 2 (Application & Analysis)**: Code tracing, historical causality analysis, problem-solving with boundary conditions.
- **Tier 3 (Synthesis & Evaluation)**: Architecture design, architectural trade-offs, proof construction, distractor analysis in MCQs.

## 3. Spaced Repetition Protocol
Log and track review intervals in `progress.md`:
- **Interval 1**: 24 hours post-lecture.
- **Interval 2**: 72 hours (3 days).
- **Interval 3**: 1 week (7 days).
- **Interval 4**: 1 month (30 days).
